Welcome

Good to Know

Cancellation Policy

Your appointment time is reserved just for you. I understand that life happens, and sometimes plans need to change. If you need to cancel or reschedule, I kindly ask that you provide at least 24 hours' notice.

  • Cancellations or reschedules made 24 hours or more before your appointment will not incur a fee.
  • Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged 50% of the scheduled service.
  • No-shows or same-day cancellations may be charged 100% of the scheduled service.
  • Clients who repeatedly cancel with little notice may be required to prepay future appointments.

If you are feeling sick or experiencing an emergency, please reach out as soon as possible. I understand that unexpected situations happen, and exceptions may be made at my discretion.

Thank you for respecting my time and supporting my small business. Your understanding allows me to continue providing a relaxing, personalized experience for every client.
